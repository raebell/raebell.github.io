Museums

      • Collected animal data for internal record keeping and annual USDA reports
      • Monitored volunteer schedules, checked in volunteers, worked with volunteers to ensure knowledge of exhibition
      • Staffed museum's Butterfly Vivarium, overseeing visitors, volunteers, and insects to ensure safety of all people and animals
  • Brooklyn Childrens Museum
    • Collections Assistant
      • Assists with placement and transportation of collections objects during exhibition switchovers
      • Creates TMS records for newly acquired collections objects, including phtography, documenting physical description, and light provenence research
      • Assists with physical organization of collections objects
      • Records location and status information in TMS for objects being loaned to or returned from other institutions, schools, or programs
    • School Programs Educator
      • Tought histroy, science, and art programming to school groups visiting BCM
      • Collaborated with education team on development of new classes and programming
      • Prepared all program materials, displays, and activities
  • South Street Seaport Museum
    • Ship Restoration Volunteer
      • Assists with maintencance and restoration of 1885 Tall Ship Wavertree
      • Works in metalshop, woodshop, fancywork, and rigging
